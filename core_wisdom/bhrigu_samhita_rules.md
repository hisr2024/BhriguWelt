# Bhrigu Samhita - Core Principles and Rules

## Introduction to Bhrigu Samhita

The Bhrigu Samhita is one of the most revered texts in Vedic astrology, attributed to Maharishi Bhrigu, one of the Saptarishis (seven great sages). This sacred manuscript contains detailed life predictions based on planetary positions at birth, spanning past, present, and future incarnations.

**Historical Context:**
- Compiled over 5,000 years ago by Maharishi Bhrigu
- Contains predictions for millions of souls across different time periods
- Written on palm leaves in ancient Sanskrit
- Preserved through generations of Brahmin families
- Centers exist primarily in Hoshiarpur (Punjab), Tamil Nadu, and Gujarat

**Fundamental Philosophy:**
The Bhrigu Samhita operates on the principle that every soul's karmic journey is pre-written based on their past actions (karma). The positions of planets at birth reveal this karmic blueprint with remarkable precision.
