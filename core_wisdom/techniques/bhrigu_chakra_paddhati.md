# Bhrigu Chakra Paddhati — F-BCP-01 (label: tradition-attributed)

**What it is:**
- Each year of life activates one house, counted from the Lagna. Running year N activates house `((N − 1) mod 12) + 1`, from one birthday to the next.
- The occupants and lord of that house describe the year.

**Where it comes from:**
- It is taught in modern Bhrigu-school material, for example the *Bhrighu Saral Paddhati* series published by Saptarishis Astrology.
- We found no classical verse that defines it.
- It resembles the classical annual-progression ideas of profection-type counting, but that similarity is not a citation.

**How the app uses it:**
- Weight 0.5, with a "tradition" badge.
- It is down-weighted when the birth time is approximate.
- When the birth time is unknown, the house is counted from the Moon instead of the Lagna.
