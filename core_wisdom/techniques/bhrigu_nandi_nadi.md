# Bhrigu Nandi Nadi and the stellar rules

## Bhrigu Nandi Nadi (BNN) — label: tradition-attributed

**Background:**
- Nadi granthas are palm-leaf texts preserved mostly in Tamil Nadu (for example the Vaitheeswaran Koil Nadi readers).
- BNN, a Nadi text in Sanskrit, reached a wide readership through R. G. Rao's English edition in the late 20th century.
- Its working principles are known mainly from that edition and from later teaching. Treat them as a living tradition, not as a dated manuscript.

| ID | Rule as implemented |
|---|---|
| F-BNN-02 | The **jiva karaka** is Jupiter for a male chart and Venus for a female chart. Its links are planets with it, in trine (1/5/9), and in the 2nd, 12th and 7th signs. It needs the optional gender field. If gender is "prefer not to say", the rule is skipped, never guessed. |
| F-BNN-03 | **Saturn is the karma karaka.** Its sign links describe work and karma. A retrograde planet also acts from the previous sign. |
| F-BNN-01 | **Jupiter progresses one sign per year** from its natal sign. Natal planets linked to the progressed sign are "activated" that year. |

## Star-lord chain — F-STAR-01 (label: modern)
- The principle: a planet delivers the results of its nakshatra lord.
- It is common to Nadi readings and to 20th-century stellar systems such as K. S. Krishnamurti's (KP, 1960s).
- The engine follows a domain lord to its star lord, then lists the houses that star lord occupies and owns.
- KP sub-lords are not used.
