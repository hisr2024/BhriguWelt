# Bhrigu Bindu — F-BB-01 (label: tradition-attributed)

**What it is:** the midpoint from Rahu to the natal Moon, measured forward:
`BB = Rahu + ((Moon − Rahu) mod 360) / 2`.
Transits of Saturn and Jupiter over this point are read as event triggers.

**Where it comes from:**
- The name ties it to Maharishi Bhrigu, but no verse defining it has been traced in any printed Bhrigu Samhita, BPHS or other classical hora text.
- It is discussed in 20th-century Indian astrological writing. The earliest author we have seen cited is C. S. Patel, in articles on Bhrigu Bindu transits.
- P. V. R. Narasimha Rao has published experiments with a progressed Bhrigu Bindu and calls the progression method "hit or miss".

**How the app uses it:**
- Transits over the point, with a 1° orb, over the next 10 years.
- The weight is 0.6, below classical dasha and gochara rules.
- The astrologer view shows the "tradition" badge.
