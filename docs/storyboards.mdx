# Animation storyboards

## KIAAN animated logo
- **Intro (0–800ms):** Flute fades in from left with shimmer sweep; feather rises from baseline with slight sway.
- **Idle (800ms+):** Feather sways gently; shimmer cycles every 4s; ripple expands behind wordmark.
- **Hover/Focus:** Increase aura glow and ripple contrast; subtitle brightens.
- **Active:** Wordmark emboss increases, ripple tightens to the selected vertex; shimmer speeds to 3s.
- **Exit:** Ripple fades out first, feather holds for 200ms then eases down.

## MindVibe app icon ripple
- **Intro:** Icon scales from 0.92→1 with Glow bloom; inner orb brightens.
- **Idle:** Dual pulses offset by 1.2s; background gradient subtly shifts hue.
- **Hover:** Pulses accelerate to 3.4s loops; add soft outline.
- **Active:** Central orb scales to 1.1 with slight white flash; return to idle within 300ms.
- **Exit:** Fade pulses while maintaining base gradient.

## Triangle flow system
- **Intro:** Energy outline draws around triangle in 900ms; sparks take their orbit positions.
- **Idle:** Flow rotates slowly; sparks orbit on staggered loops.
- **Hover:** Energy leans toward hovered vertex; glow intensifies on that node.
- **Active:** Active node scales to 1.05, leg toward node thickens; pulse node-specific color.
- **Exit:** Outline relaxes back to even weight; sparks slow to idle pace.

## Peacock feather sway
- **Intro:** Feather stems in from right with 6px motion blur; aura fades in.
- **Idle:** 6s sway loop; aura pulses.
- **Hover/Focus:** Increase sway amplitude slightly; eye highlight brightens.
- **Active:** Feather holds upright for 200ms then returns to loop.
- **Exit:** Aura dims and feather eases downward with gravity-like pull.

## Flute shimmer
- **Intro:** Flute slides up 8px with opacity fade-in.
- **Idle:** Shimmer sweeps every 4s; tail shimmer follows at 1.2s delay.
- **Hover:** Shimmer speed increases; metallic gradient gains contrast.
- **Active:** Short burst shimmer (2s) then settle into idle cadence.
- **Exit:** Gradient desaturates; flute fades down.
