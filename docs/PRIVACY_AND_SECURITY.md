## 🔒 Encryption: Protecting Your Sensitive Data

### What Is Encryption?

Think of encryption like a secure vault. Your personal information is locked with a special key that only you have (your passcode). Even if someone gets access to your phone, they can't read your data without your passcode.

### How We Encrypt Your Data

**Technical Details (for those interested):**
- **Algorithm**: AES-256-GCM (military-grade encryption)
- **Key Derivation**: Your passcode creates a unique encryption key using PBKDF2 with 100,000 rounds
- **Random Salt**: Each installation gets unique random data to make your encryption even stronger
- **Fresh IV**: Every piece of data is encrypted with a unique random value

**What This Means for You:**
- 🔐 Your passcode creates a unique encryption key
- 🛡️ All sensitive data is scrambled before being saved
- 🔑 Only your passcode can unscramble it
- 💪 The encryption is so strong, it would take billions of years to break

### What Is End-to-End Encryption?

**Simple explanation**: From the moment you enter information to the moment you view it again, your data is encrypted. There's no moment where it sits unprotected on your device.

**How it works in BhriguWelt**:

1. **You enter your passcode** → Creates encryption key in your device's memory
2. **You add birth details** → Instantly encrypted before saving
3. **Data saved to device** → Stored in encrypted form only
4. **App closes or locks** → Encryption key is erased from memory
5. **You open app again** → Enter passcode to recreate encryption key
6. **View your data** → Decrypted only in memory, for viewing

The encrypted data on your device is useless without your passcode. Even we (the developers) cannot decrypt it.

## ⚠️ Security Limitations: What You Should Know

We believe in **honest transparency** about what our app can and cannot protect.

### Browser Security Limitations

#### 1. Active Session Memory
**What it means**: While you're using the app, decrypted data exists temporarily in your device's memory.

**The reality**: 
- ✅ When app is locked, no sensitive data in memory
- ⚠️ While viewing a report, that report is decrypted in memory
- ✅ Switching apps or locking clears memory
- ⚠️ Screen recording or screenshot tools can capture what's visible

**What you can do**: 
- Lock the app when not actively using it
- Enable auto-lock (Settings → Security → Auto-lock after 2 minutes)
- Don't use the app on shared or public devices

#### 2. Browser Developer Tools
**What it means**: Chrome/Firefox developer tools can inspect web app memory.

**The reality**:
- ⚠️ If someone has physical access while app is unlocked, they could use developer tools
- ✅ Requires both device access AND app to be unlocked
- ✅ Passcode protects against this when locked
- ❌ No protection against screen recording apps while in use

**What you can do**:
- Keep your device locked when not in use
- Use device-level passcode/biometrics
- Don't share your device while app is unlocked
- Log out when lending your device to others

#### 3. Device Backups
**What it means**: Your device's backup system (iCloud, Google Drive) may copy your encrypted data.

**The reality**:
- ✅ Data is encrypted in backups (useless without passcode)
- ✅ Encryption keys are NOT backed up (by design)
- ⚠️ If someone gets your backup AND your passcode, they can access data
- ✅ Restoring to new device requires passcode to decrypt

**What you can do**:
- Use strong device backup passwords
- Keep passcode separate from backup password
- Consider excluding BhriguWelt from automatic backups (device settings)

#### 4. Screen Recording and Screenshots
**What it means**: System-level screen capture can record decrypted content while viewing.

**The reality**:
- ⚠️ Screen recording apps can capture whatever you see
- ⚠️ Screenshots can capture open reports
- ❌ Browser apps cannot prevent system-level screen capture
- ✅ Native mobile apps (iOS/Android) can block screenshots

**What you can do**:
- Be mindful in public spaces
- Lock app when sharing screen
- Review device permissions for screen recording apps
- Consider using native mobile app (when available)

### What Could Go Wrong?

We want you to understand realistic risks:

#### Device Theft or Loss
- **If locked**: ✅ Data is encrypted, inaccessible without passcode
- **If unlocked**: ⚠️ Data is accessible until auto-lock triggers
- **Prevention**: Enable auto-lock (2-5 minutes), use device passcode

#### Forgotten Passcode
- **Can you recover it?**: ❌ **No.** There is no password reset or recovery.
- **Why not?**: Your passcode generates the encryption key. Without it, data cannot be decrypted. Ever.
- **Prevention**: Choose a memorable passcode. Consider writing it down and storing safely offline.

#### Malware on Device
- **If device is compromised**: ⚠️ Malware could capture passcode when you enter it
- **If app is locked**: ✅ Encrypted data remains protected
- **Prevention**: Keep device OS updated, avoid suspicious apps, use antivirus

#### Physical Access While Unlocked
- **If someone has your unlocked device**: ⚠️ They can view all data until auto-lock
- **Prevention**: Lock manually when stepping away, enable shortest auto-lock time

### What We Do to Protect You

Despite browser limitations, we implement:

- ✅ **Automatic encryption** of all sensitive data
- ✅ **Memory clearing** when app locks
- ✅ **Auto-lock timers** (configurable)
- ✅ **No persistent keys** (recreated from passcode each time)
- ✅ **No cloud storage** (nothing to hack remotely)
- ✅ **Minimal attack surface** (offline-first design)
- ✅ **Regular security updates** (watch for app updates)

### What We Cannot Protect Against

For honesty and transparency:

- ❌ **Physical device access** while unlocked
- ❌ **Screen recording** by other apps
- ❌ **Keylogging** if device has malware
- ❌ **Backup theft** if passcode is also compromised
- ❌ **Browser developer tools** while in active use
- ❌ **Memory dumps** while app is unlocked (requires sophisticated attack)

## ❓ FAQ: Questions From Real Users

### General Privacy

#### "Can I use this app without the internet?"
**Yes!** BhriguWelt works 100% offline. Install once, use forever, no internet required.

#### "Who can see my data?"
**Only you.** Your data is encrypted on your device. We (the developers) cannot see it. Your ISP cannot see it. Cloud services cannot see it. Nobody but you.

#### "Do you track what I do in the app?"
**No.** We don't use analytics, tracking pixels, or telemetry. We have no idea how you use the app, and that's intentional.

#### "Can someone hack my account?"
**There is no account.** Everything is local to your device. There's no online account to hack, no servers to breach, no database with your information.

### Passcode & Security

#### "What happens if I forget my passcode?"
**You cannot recover your data.** This is a security feature, not a bug. Your passcode is the ONLY way to decrypt your data. Without it, the encryption is unbreakable.

**Options if you forget:**
- Try all passcodes you might have used
- Check if you wrote it down anywhere
- If truly lost: Settings → Advanced → Clear All Data (starts fresh)

**Prevention:**
- Choose a memorable passcode
- Write it down and store in a safe place (physically, not digitally)
- Consider a pattern that has personal meaning

#### "Is a 4-digit passcode secure enough?"
**It depends.** For most users, yes. But longer is better.

- **4 digits**: 10,000 possible combinations - Good for casual protection
- **6 digits**: 1,000,000 combinations - Better for sensitive data
- **8+ digits**: 100,000,000+ combinations - Strongest protection

We recommend 6 digits as a balance between security and memorability.

#### "Can I change my passcode?"
**Yes**, in Settings → Security → Change Passcode. You'll need:
1. Current passcode (to decrypt data)
2. New passcode (to re-encrypt data)

This process re-encrypts all your data with the new passcode.

#### "What's auto-lock and should I use it?"
**Auto-lock** automatically locks the app after you stop using it. **Yes, definitely use it!**

When you lock your device or close the app, BhriguWelt:
- Clears encryption keys from memory
- Requires passcode to access again
- Protects your data if you leave device unattended

**Recommended setting**: 2-5 minutes

### Data Safety

#### "Is my data safe if I lose my device?"
**Yes**, as long as:
- ✅ You had a passcode set (required on first use)
- ✅ Auto-lock is enabled (enabled by default)
- ✅ Your device itself has a lock screen (recommended)

Without your passcode, the encrypted data is useless to whoever finds your device.

**But note**: If your device was unlocked when lost, data is accessible until auto-lock triggers. This is why we recommend a short auto-lock timeout.

#### "What if someone steals my device while unlocked?"
They can access your data until auto-lock triggers (2-5 minutes by default).

**To minimize risk**:
- Lock manually when stepping away (tap lock icon)
- Use shortest auto-lock time you can tolerate
- Enable device-level security (Face ID, fingerprint, etc.)

#### "Can I back up my data?"
**Yes!** Settings → Data Management → Export Data

This creates an encrypted backup file (JSON format):
- ✅ Data is encrypted in the backup
- ✅ Requires your passcode to restore
- ✅ Can transfer to new device
- ⚠️ Keep backup file secure (it contains encrypted data)
- ⚠️ Keep passcode separate from backup file

#### "What happens if my device breaks?"
**If you have a backup**: Restore to new device using your backup file and passcode.

**If you don't have a backup**: Your data is lost. We cannot recover it (we don't have it).

**Prevention**: Export backups regularly (Settings → Export Data) and store safely.

### Troubleshooting

#### "The app won't unlock with my passcode"
**Possible causes**:
1. Typing error (try again carefully)
2. Different passcode than you remember
3. Caps Lock on (if using numeric + letters)
4. Device keyboard language changed

**After 5 failed attempts**: 30-second timeout (prevents brute force attacks)

**If truly forgotten**: Unfortunately, must clear data and start fresh.

#### "Auto-lock is too aggressive"
Change in Settings → Security → Auto-lock Timeout:
- 1 minute (maximum security)
- 2 minutes (balanced - recommended)
- 5 minutes (relaxed)
- 10 minutes (convenience)
- Never (not recommended)

#### "App is slow to unlock"
**This is intentional.** Deriving the encryption key from your passcode takes ~500ms to 1 second. This delay:
- ✅ Strengthens security (makes brute force attacks impractical)
- ✅ Is normal and expected
- ✅ Happens only on unlock, not during use

**On desktop** (Chrome/Edge):
1. Look for ➕ or 💻 icon in address bar
2. Or: Menu → Install BhriguWelt
3. Or: Settings → Apps → Install

**On mobile** (Safari/Chrome):
1. Tap **Share** icon
2. Select **Add to Home Screen**
3. Confirm installation

Some browsers on some devices don't support installation (use as web app instead).

#### "Updates aren't working"
The app checks for updates automatically. If you see "Update Available":
1. Close all BhriguWelt tabs

3. Reopen app
4. Update applies automatically

If stuck: Settings → Advanced → Clear Cache (doesn't delete your data)
