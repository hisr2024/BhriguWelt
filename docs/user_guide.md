# BhriguWelt User Guide

This guide walks seekers and practitioners through the core flows of the platform.

## Finding your way
- **Navigation:** The homepage anchors every form with clear anchors (horoscope, calendar, matchmaking).
- **Language toggle:** Use the bilingual toggle in the toolbar to swap copy between English and Hindi without reloading.
- **Accessibility:** Large tap targets, aria labels, and high-contrast type keep the interface usable on mobile.

## Horoscope requests
1. Open the **Horoscope** panel and enter name, birth date, birth time, and place.
2. Optional: include lunar tithi, bhava placements, and modern preferences for richer insights.
3. Submit to receive interpreted weights, remedies, past-life glimpses, and future trajectories.

## Calendar conversion
- The Śaka converter accepts Gregorian dates and outputs tithi, nakshatra, and festival-friendly timings.
- Use the downloadable PDF to share timings with seekers in bilingual copy.

## Matchmaking
- Provide **primary** and **partner** details with lifestyle preferences.
- Review compatibility indices and modern highlights before exporting suggested rituals.

## Chat and insights
- The **Bhrigu chat** widget answers clarifying questions in real time.
- Quarterly review summaries track feedback across cohorts so facilitators know which principles resonate.
