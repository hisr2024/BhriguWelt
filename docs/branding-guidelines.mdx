# MindVibe Companion Brand Guidelines

## Identity pillars
- Calming, premium, emotionally intelligent.
- Universal gradients: Sunrise (orange–gold), Ocean (teal–sky), Aurora (rose–lilac).
- KIAAN — MindVibe Companion tagline: **Crisp, calm guidance.**

## Color tokens
- **mvGradientSunrise:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #ffb16f, #ffd98e)`
- **mvGradientOcean:** `linear-gradient(145deg, #1fb4c6, #7ae2ff)`
- **mvGradientAurora:** `linear-gradient(140deg, #f28abf, #b9b3ff)`
- **Inner Peace Primary:** `#35c0c2`
- **Mind Control Primary:** `#1a4b9b`
- **Self Kindness Primary:** `#e18ad1`
- Text `#0e1b27`, Muted `#4b5563`, Border `#dfe7f1`, Surface `#ffffff`, Canvas `#0c1528`.

### Light / Dark
- Light background `#f7fbff`, dark background `#0c1528`.
- Maintain WCAG AA contrast for body text (minimum 4.5:1). Buttons use 16px text or heavier weight for AA Large.

## Typography
- Fonts: **Plus Jakarta Sans** (display, UI), **Manrope** (body).
- Hierarchy: Display (clamp 2.8–3.6rem), H1 (2.2–2.8rem), H2 (1.8–2.2rem), H3 (1.5–1.8rem), H4 (1.35rem), H5 (1.12rem), Body (1rem), Microcopy (0.9rem), Labels (0.85rem).
- Letter-spacing: wordmarks `0.04em`, labels `0.08em`.

## Iconography
- Rounded corners (12–16px), stroke weight 1.5–2px, soft glow edges.
- MindVibe icon uses concentric ovals with a central orb; badges apply Sunrise/Ocean/Aurora gradients.

## Spacing & sizing
- Scale (px): 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48.
- Radii: pill 999px, soft 14px, card 18px, badge 12px.
- Shadows: card `0 18px 48px rgba(5,30,73,0.12)`, soft `0 12px 28px rgba(5,30,73,0.08)`, glow `0 0 38px rgba(240,141,182,0.32)`.

## Glow & shadow specs
- Use radial glows to hint motion: feather aura `radial-gradient(circle, rgba(240,141,182,0.4), transparent 60%)`.
- Avoid harsh outlines; borders stay at `#dfe7f1`.

## Accessibility
- Preserve focus states with gradient-backed outlines.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by switching animated assets to the static KIAAN lockup.
- Minimum tap targets 44px for primary actions.

## Logo usage
- **MindVibeIcon** for app badges, sized 32–64px.
- **MindVibeWordmark** for headers; pair with Sunrise/Ocean/Aurora gradients.
- **KiaanLogoAnimated** for hero/topbar; fallback to **KiaanLogoStatic** on low power.
- Keep clear space equal to the height of the “K” around the lockup.
