## [4] Location-by-Location Analysis (General Template)
For each city/country you are evaluating, reflect using the prompts below.

### Label Examples
- "Career-Expansion Zone" if the place offers visibility, industry hubs, or mentors (10th/9th activation).
- "Relationship/Network Zone" if social warmth, collaboration, or partnership vibes are strong (7th/11th).
- "Healing/Retreat Zone" if it feels quieter, nature-rich, or invites introspection (12th/4th/8th).
- "Study/Exploration Zone" if it inspires learning or travel (9th/3rd).

### Bullet Prompts per Location
- **Career:** Are there industry clusters, mentors, or visibility pathways? Do you feel more disciplined or more experimental here?
- **Relationships:** Do connections form easily? Is there a cooperative or competitive tone? Partnerships stable or transient?
- **Emotional feel:** Do you feel settled, nostalgic, or restless? Is the housing/support system comfortable (4th house tone)?
- **Spiritual tone:** Does the place invite practice, contemplation, pilgrimage, or service (12th/9th)?
- **Supports:** note infrastructure, community, mentors, safety, and affordability.
- **Challenges:** note bureaucracy, isolation risk, cost of living, or cultural adjustment.

## [5] Comparative Summary & Recommendations

- Identify which location encourages **inner growth/peace** (12th/9th/4th qualities).
- Prioritize 1–3 options based on your main motive (career, study, relationship, spiritual retreat). Keep a runner-up for balance.

## [6] Risks & Cautions for Moving
- Emotional strain and adjustment time; allow for homesickness and cultural learning curves.
- Financial planning: budget for housing, visas, taxes, and a 3–6 month buffer.
- Isolation risk: pre-join communities or groups to anchor socially.
- Bureaucracy and visas: seek qualified professional guidance; timelines may be long.
- No place is perfect; each carries a karma to work through.

## [7] Suggested Approach to Relocation (Inner & Outer)
- **Inner:** Clarify intention; keep a reflective journal on how each place feels during visits. Cultivate flexibility and patience.
- **Outer:** Do scouting trips, speak with locals, map commute/housing costs, test internet and daily routines. Have contingency plans.
- **Spiritual:** Offer gratitude to your current place; set a sankalpa (resolve) for the move; honor that free will plus grace shapes outcomes.
